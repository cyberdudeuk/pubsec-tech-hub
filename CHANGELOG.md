# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (24 November 2026)

First release, rebuilt from the Figma Make prototype "GovTech Framework Development".

- App store with 12 curated listings, each with owner, licence, code, live service and sourced facts; no ratings or reviews.
- Weekly publiccode.yml harvest that never copies contact details.
- Issue forms to list, claim or declare use, plus contact, correction and accessibility forms.
- Developers, directories and ecosystem sections, keeping the prototype's genuine links and dropping its invented numbers.
- Search across the hub and an Ask assistant that answers only from the hub's own content.
- Glossary, knowledge graph, data dictionary, requirements traced to user needs and checks, and the prototype reconciliation register.
- 20 static checks, 12 in-page self-checks and unit tests, each with a negative test; automated visual inspection in CI.
